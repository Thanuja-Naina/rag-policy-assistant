# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward to the next year; the remaining days lapse on 31 March.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required if sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

## Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave. Paternity leave is 10 working days of paid leave and must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.

## Leave Approval
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance. Managers must approve or reject requests within 2 working days. Emergency leave may be applied for retrospectively within 3 days of returning to work.

## Public Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays each year; the list is published in January. Employees who work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off within 30 days.

## Unpaid Leave
Unpaid leave of up to 30 days may be granted after 1 year of service, subject to director approval.
