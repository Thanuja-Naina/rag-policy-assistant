# Expense and Travel Policy

## Travel Reimbursement
Employees are reimbursed for business travel booked through the corporate travel desk. Economy class is mandatory for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours with VP approval. Hotel stays are capped at 8,000 INR per night in metro cities and 5,000 INR per night elsewhere.

## Meal Allowance
The daily meal allowance during business travel is 1,500 INR. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Claim Submission
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date together with original receipts. Claims above 25,000 INR need approval from a department head. Late claims are rejected unless Finance approves them.

## Reimbursement Timeline
Approved claims are paid within 10 working days through payroll direct deposit.

## Client Entertainment
Client entertainment expenses up to 3,000 INR per person need prior manager approval. Gifts to clients are limited to 2,000 INR per client per year.

## Mobile and Internet
Employees on the remote work plan receive a monthly internet reimbursement of 1,000 INR. Mobile bills are reimbursed up to 600 INR per month for client-facing roles.

## Mileage
Use of a personal car for business travel is reimbursed at 12 INR per km. Two-wheelers are reimbursed at 5 INR per km.
