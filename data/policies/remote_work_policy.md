# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed 6 months of service and whose role is not on-site only are eligible for remote work. Eligibility is confirmed by the manager and HR.

## Hybrid Schedule
The standard hybrid schedule is 3 days in the office and 2 days remote per week. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 local time, during which employees must be reachable.

## Fully Remote
Fully remote arrangements require approval from the VP of the department and are reviewed every 6 months.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home office stipend of 15,000 INR. Equipment must be returned within 7 days of leaving the company.

## Working From Another Location
Working from another city within the country is permitted for up to 20 days per year with manager approval. Working from abroad is not permitted without HR and legal approval because of tax and compliance requirements.

## Security Requirements
Remote employees must connect through the company VPN and use multi-factor authentication. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN enabled.

## Performance and Availability
Remote employees must attend team stand-ups on video and respond to messages within 2 hours during working hours.
