# Data Security Policy

## Password Requirements
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and changed every 90 days. Reuse of the last 12 passwords is not allowed. Password managers approved by IT are recommended.

## Data Classification
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Customer personal data is always Restricted. Restricted data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.

## Incident Reporting
Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within 1 hour of discovery through the security hotline or the incident portal. Employees must not attempt to investigate on their own.

## Removable Media
USB storage devices are blocked by default. Exceptions require written approval from the IT security manager and the device must be encrypted.

## Phishing
Employees must report suspicious emails using the Report Phishing button. Phishing awareness training is mandatory once per year and takes about 45 minutes.

## Data Retention
Customer records are retained for 7 years after account closure, after which they must be securely deleted. System logs are retained for 12 months.

## Third-Party Sharing
Sharing Confidential or Restricted data with third parties requires a signed NDA and approval from the data owner and the legal team.
